## About Me

I’m a UK-based Game QA Tester providing structured QA support for indie games across PC, mobile, and VR.

My work focuses on gameplay systems, onboarding clarity, usability, accessibility-related observations, issue reproduction, regression testing, and player-facing issues that affect clarity, progression, or release readiness.

I currently support Loom & Lore Games. Released projects include **Mama Llama's Food Cart Adventure**, **Dungeon Raiders**, and **Nothing Strange Here**. Current projects include **Dungeon Raiders EX** and **Prime Time: Slime Time**.

## My QA Focus

### Risk-Focused QA Planning

Scoping test passes around active builds, recent updates, and player-facing risk.

### Exploratory Systems Testing

Testing gameplay systems, progression flow, recent changes, and likely failure points.

### Developer-Ready Reporting

Clear bug reports with reproduction steps, evidence, gameplay impact, and practical recommendations.

### Player-Facing Quality Analysis

Usability, onboarding, accessibility, readability, and gameplay clarity observations.

* Plan focused QA passes around active builds, recent updates, and player-facing risk
